## Brand & Style

This design system translates traditional Thai street culinary craft—specifically charcoal-grilled bananas drenched in slow-simmered palm sugar and coconut caramel—into an elevated contemporary digital storefront. 

The aesthetic marries **Tactile Modernism** with **Appetite-Driven Editorial Design**:
- **Personality:** Welcoming, artisanal, steeped in heritage yet immaculately clean and contemporary. It communicates warmth, smoke, molten sweetness, and meticulous freshness.
- **Audience:** Urban foodies, nostalgic locals, cafe hoppers, and dessert enthusiasts who seek authentic culinary roots paired with modern service reliability and aesthetic delight.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, sensory cravings, tactile warmth, and instantaneous hunger appeal.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the physical elements of charcoal street cooking: molten palm nectar, fire-toasted banana skins, rich coconut milk, and fragrant banana leaf wraps.

- **Primary (`#D97706` / Honey Golden Amber):** The anchor hue for high-intent CTAs, active highlights, and caramel glaze accents. Supports `#F59E0B` for vibrant hover/focus states.
- **Secondary (`#78350F` / Roasted Coconut Caramel):** Evokes caramelized sugars and toasted coconuts; used for premium headers, sub-banners, and structural framing.
- **Tertiary (`#15803D` / Banana Leaf Green):** Fresh, vegetal accent representing pure ingredients, organic quality labels, vegan/signature badges, and checkout successes.
- **Neutral Deep (`#451A03` / Charred Sugarwood):** Deep, warm espresso-charcoal used in place of sterile black for ultra-legible typography, borders, and tactile icons.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFDF5` / Coconut Cream):** Warm off-white foundation that eliminates cold digital sterility and replicates the soft warmth of fresh coconut cream.

## Typography

The type scale pairs **Epilogue** (for sculptural, editorial headlines with rhythmic curves that mirror roasted geometries) with **Plus Jakarta Sans** (for friendly, rounded, crystal-clear body copy and navigation). 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold and extra-bold weights of Epilogue. Tight tracking conveys confidence, richness, and culinary tradition.
- **Body & Labels:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Its generous x-height and open counters guarantee effortless readability across ordering screens, recipe descriptions, and nutrition labels.
- **Thai Complementary Pairings:** In production Thai localization, map Epilogue to *Kanit* or *Prompt* (SemiBold/Bold) for headlines, and map Plus Jakarta Sans to *Prompt* or *Sarabun* for long-form narrative body.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a flexible, 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by an 8px spatial rhythm:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 48px outer canvas margins (`margin`), and 24px column gutters (`gutter`). Maximum content width is capped at 1280px for balanced viewing ergonomics.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 32px outer canvas margins, and 20px gutters. Dual-pane ordering flow (menu listing alongside sticky order drawer).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px outer margins (`margin-mobile`), and 16px gutters (`gutter-mobile`). Visual priority is placed on sticky bottom bar CTAs and edge-to-edge culinary photography.
- **Rhythm Rules:** Component internals rely strictly on `space-xs` through `space-md`, while major card gaps and editorial sections employ `space-lg` to `space-xl` to ensure appetizing imagery has room to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, caramel-tinted ambient shadows and tonal surface layering to simulate soft physical warmth rather than digital elevation:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: `#FFFDF5` (Coconut Cream)
  - Elevated Container: `#FEF3C7` (Soft Palm Amber Tint, 40% opacity)
  - Highlight Container: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White, applied sparingly to bring food photography forward)
- **Shadow Philosophy:** Shadows are warm-tinted using deep charcoal-caramel (`#451A03`) rather than cold gray or black.
  - *Low Elevation (Menu Cards, Chips):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.04)`
  - *Medium Elevation (Cart Floaters, Active Cards):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.10), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.06)`
  - *High Elevation (Modals, Slide-over Menus):* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.18), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.08)`
- **Outlines:** Subtle, warm borders (`1px solid rgba(120, 53, 15, 0.12)`) maintain structural clarity when placed over bright photography.

## Shapes

The design uses a rounded geometry level (`2`), reflecting the natural, organic shape of sliced and pressed grilled bananas, warm round bowls, and clay charcoal stoves.

- **Standard Base (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Input fields, inline alert boxes, and secondary buttons.
- **Large Base (`1rem` / 16px):** Product cards, checkout item cards, and order receipt containers.
- **Extra Large Base (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Promotional banners, modal sheets, and food showcase carousels.
- **Pill Treatment (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status badges, tags (e.g., "Charcoal Grilled", "Extra Dip"), and round icon buttons to convey friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#D97706` background, `#FFFDF5` bold text, pill-rounded or `12px` border radius. On hover: shifts to `#F59E0B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.35)`). Active state scales slightly down (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary (Caramel Outline):** Transparent background with `1.5px` border in `#78350F`, text in `#78350F`. Hover fills with `rgba(120, 53, 15, 0.06)`.
- **Tertiary (Leaf Green Ghost):** `#15803D` text with light green hover wash (`rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.08)`), applied to order re-order or nutrition modals.

### Chips & Badges
- **Taste Profile Badges:** Pill-shaped with subtle tinted backgrounds:
  - *Signature / Charcoal Baked:* Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#78350F`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`.
  - *Fresh Leaf / Vegan:* Background `rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.10)`, text `#15803D`, border `1px solid rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.20)`.
  - *Spicy / Seasonal:* Background `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.10)`, text `#991B1B`.
- **Selection Chips (Portion Sizing):** Unselected state sits in `#FFFDF5` with `1px solid rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.15)`. Selected state shifts to `#78350F` with `#FFFDF5` text.

### Cards
- **Product Menu Card:** Cream background (`#FFFFFF` resting against `#FFFDF5` canvas), `16px` border-radius, warm low-elevation ambient drop shadow, and a `1px` subtle rim (`rgba(120, 53, 15, 0.08)`). Imagery occupies the top half with subtle zoom on hover. Includes an embedded round floating "+" CTA button in `#D97706`.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Light cream background (`#FEF3C7` at 25% opacity), border `1.5px solid rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.15)`, text in `#451A03`. Focus state switches border to `#D97706` with a soft `0 0 0 4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)` focus ring.
- **Radio & Checkboxes:** Circular and soft-cornered square inputs in `#D97706` with white checkmarks. Unchecked borders tinted in `#78350F` at 40% opacity.

### Lists & Quantity Pickers
- **Topping Customizer Row:** Clean horizontal list item separated by hairline dividers (`rgba(69, 26, 3, 0.06)`). Quantity pickers utilize connected pill selectors with solid tap targets and warm tactile feedback.
